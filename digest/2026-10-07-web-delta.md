### 3. OpenAI x Ironclad: computer-use training on real enterprise software
- **Source / ET:** OpenAI, published Oct 6 23:53 UTC = **7:53 PM ET** per page metadata (RSS says 10:00 UTC): https://openai.com/index/advancing-computer-use-with-ironclad/
  - Coverage: Seeking Alpha; AICoder (Oct 7)
- **Facts:**
  - 11 legal, procurement and commercial tasks (NDA setup, threshold approvals, jurisdiction-aware clauses), each graded on 8–50 criteria. Each takes an experienced human about 30–40 minutes.
  - Training used hosted Ironclad environments plus RL on synthetic tasks built from public SEC EDGAR contracts, per AICoder. No customer data.
  - **Results:**
    - GPT-6 Astra 55.0% vs GPT-5.6 Sol 41.6% (≈32% relative).
    - Time per attempt 37.0 → 19.2 minutes. These are simulated times, not measured customer savings.
    - An internal model reached 63.7%.
  - OpenAI is inviting "a small number of software companies" to bring workflows.
  - Context: Ironclad co-founder Jason Boehmig joined OpenAI in 2026 to lead a legal vertical.
- **Sentiment/buzz:** Moderate. Bullish, with caveats.
- **Tag:** TECHNICAL.
- **Messaging Themes:** maybe.
- **NetSuite note:** This is a template for ERP-vendor/model-lab co-training. No Oracle/NetSuite participation announced.
