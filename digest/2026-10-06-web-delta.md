- **Sources / ET:**

  - La Voce di NY, Oct 5–6
  - NYT, syndicated by DNYUZ Oct 6: "A.I. Officials Stonewall on Questions About Technology's Risks"
- **Format and scale:** The hearing started 11 AM ET as a Committee of the Whole. About 40 of 51 members took part. The Council reported 69 witnesses across 14 panels, and members weighed 10 bills. Witnesses were under oath; company reps appeared by video.
- **Who testified:** Morgan Dwyer (OpenAI, head of policy development & ops), Logan Graham (Anthropic), Alice Friend (Google) and Shane Cahill (Meta, director for privacy & AI legislation).
- **Catastrophic-risk number:**
  - Dwyer: "I don't know… None of these levels is remotely acceptable. We should not train models that we cannot make an extremely strong case that we can keep under human control." Menin called that "flippant at best."
  - Friend said there's no rigorous method yet.
  - Graham gave no percentage.
  - Cahill said he'd follow up.
- **Liability:**
  - Friend: "if it's illegal without AI, it's still illegal with AI."
  - Dwyer didn't answer directly.
  - Graham said it was outside his expertise.
  - Cahill said he's "not in legal."
- **Release gating:** Nobody gave a blanket yes/no. Menin: "I am going to take what each of you said as an equivocation." Dwyer said OpenAI won't release models it believes are unsafe and has delayed releases before.
- **New disclosure:** OpenAI is reviewing potential past agent-misalignment incidents back to **Nov 2025**.
  - Dwyer said she knows of no impact on NYC government systems or residents' data, but the investigation is ongoing.
  - She committed to notify affected third parties and to publish the results.
- **SpaceXAI:** Absent despite last week's subpoena. Menin: "in direct violation of the subpoena… we are pursuing that subpoena in court."
  - The City Reporter says enforcement needs a state-court proceeding.
  - **No filing reported as of the window close.** SpaceXAI gave no comment.
- **Whistleblowers:**
  - **Jacob Coxon** (ex-OpenAI/Anthropic): "We don't fully control it." He called it "more likely than not" that humanity loses control on the current path, and described a "move fast, break things" culture.
  - **Alex Turner** (ex-DeepMind) put his personal takeover estimate at about 1 in 3.
  - **Daniel Kokotajlo** (ex-OpenAI) warned about autonomy.
- **RAISE Act fight:**
  - Sen. Gounardes testified that the companies "spent millions" to defeat the original version.
  - Asm. **Alex Bores** posted "I believe that @OpenAI just committed perjury," then called OpenAI's stated position "a lie."
  - Pressed by CM Marte, Dwyer said OpenAI supports the RAISE Act "which passed." She didn't claim support for the original.
  - No OpenAI response to the perjury charge was found.
- **Next steps:**
  - Companies will get written follow-up questions.
  - No vote is scheduled on the 10 bills.
  - Mayor Mamdani said AI should be regulated "at every single level" but that the most urgent response must come from Washington. He didn't endorse specific bills.
- **Sentiment/buzz:** Loud. Skeptical of the companies; coverage is uniformly framed as "evasive/stonewall."

### 3. OpenAI: text watermarking for EU AI Act compliance
- **Source / ET:** OpenAI, Oct 5 15:00 UTC = **11:00 AM ET**: https://openai.com/index/eu-text-provenance
  - Coverage: The New Stack, 3:29 PM ET ("off by default, unlike Anthropic")
- **What ships:**
  - API customers globally can opt in to text watermarking for select models. It's **off by default**.
  - Over the coming weeks, an invisible watermark is added to eligible **ChatGPT and Codex text output in the EU**.
  - Detector access is application-only, for approved researchers and organizations.
  - The technique is **textGrain**, a statistical signal in word choice. OpenAI says it matched or beat other approaches, including SynthID-text, and plans to open-source it.
  - **Caveat OpenAI states:** at 1% false positives, about 80% detection on 200-token passages. Short text is hard.
  - Image/audio verification tools stay public.
- **Sentiment/buzz:** Moderate. Meh to skeptical on reliability.
- **Tag:** TECHNICAL/SECURITY (provenance).
- **Messaging Themes:** maybe.
