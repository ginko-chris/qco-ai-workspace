  - Persistent GPT-6 Astra agents, each with its own cloud computer and browser.
  - Connect to 4,000+ apps through plugins.
  - Reachable in ChatGPT, Slack, Teams and by voice call; SMS "coming soon."
  - Can optionally connect to the user's laptop.
- **Controls:**
  - Background "proactive research" uses read-only tools only.
  - Custom Rules set allow, require-approval or block for specific actions.
  - Auto-review checks account-affecting actions; password changes always stay with the user.

  - Org-owned dots with their own identity, credentials and systems-of-record integrations, starting as "focused enterprise pilots." OpenAI named procurement, invoice processing, customer support and contracting as internal test areas.
  - To be managed through **Microsoft Agent 365** governance controls.
- **Demo note:** the live demo stalled ("slow morning"); per dev.to/HN, two open-source clones appeared within an hour.
- **Sentiment/buzz:** Very loud. Framed as OpenAI's answer to Meta Muse, Microsoft Autopilot and Grok Bot. Mixed: bullish on scope, skeptical after the demo hiccups and the rogue-agent context.

- **Accord (voluntary; Trump called it "morally binding," "almost like a constitution"):** each frontier company "should implement":
  - (1) internal controls monitoring capabilities and alignment in training and deployment (cyber, bio, chem), including ensuring models don't "hack or access technical systems in unintended ways"
  - (2) an internal team to verify the controls work
  - (3) an independent external auditor or evaluator
  - (4) an independent board committee overseeing the reports
  - Participants will "meet regularly to establish standards." It "may make sense to codify" over time.
- **Signers** (Washington Examiner): Pichai, Zuckerberg, Amodei, **Musk (SpaceXAI)**, Huang, Brockman.
- **Also present per reports:** Nadella, Tom Brown, Bessent, Lutnick, Wiles, Johnson. Lisa Su appears in a Reuters photo. GMA lists Karp, Shyam Sankar and Bezos.
- **Executive actions:**
  - EO: agencies must use "Super Intelligence"/"SI" instead of "AI" in non-statutory materials. The science adviser (OSTP) will draft a federal SI definition.
  - Trump is "considering" a **10-person board** to police AI safety (members unspecified) and will "soon" name a new White House AI lead.
  - He doubled down on data-center expansion ("massive contributions to communities").
- **Context:** Reuters/Ipsos (Sep 17–20) found 73% worry labs haven't done enough; 55% favor slowing.
- **Sentiment/buzz:** Very loud. Skeptical in policy press ("thin on details," voluntary). Bullish on the right ("innovation-first").
- **Tag:** **POLITICAL-AMPLIFICATION.**
- **MESSAGING THEMES candidate:** **yes.** Voluntary self-regulation vs. the Warner/Schatz bill and the SAFA body. It echoes the labs' own SAFA idea; the "SI" rebrand is agenda-driven framing.
- **Grok Bot beat note:** Musk signed. It's SpaceXAI's first public commitment to the external-audit and board-committee layers.
