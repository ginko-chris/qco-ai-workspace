### 1. OpenAI and Ironclad co-train agents on a real business app — **Score 3/5**
**BLUF:** This is OpenAI's first partnership training agents on a real business software product (Ironclad does contract management). Training used synthetic tasks built from public SEC contracts, not customer data. On 11 legal and procurement tasks, GPT-6 Astra scored 55.0%, GPT-5.6 Sol 41.6%, and an internal model 63.7%. Time per attempt fell from 37.0 to 19.2 minutes, but **those times are simulated**. OpenAI is inviting more software vendors.
**Why:** This is probably how NetSuite agents will arrive: a lab co-trains with the vendor. Even the best model fails about a third of the tasks.
**Not said:** No real customer outcomes, no error costs, no Oracle involvement.
**Hype:** Moderate. **Sentiment:** Moderate, bullish.
**Actionable:** Vendor checklist: for any agent built into a business app (NetSuite at SuiteWorld Oct 25–28), ask for measured customer results, not simulated time, plus the task failure rate, and confirm whether customer data is used in training. Human gates stay; a 55–64% pass rate is draft-only territory.
