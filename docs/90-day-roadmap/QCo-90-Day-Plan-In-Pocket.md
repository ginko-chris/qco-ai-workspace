## Four pillars (how I’ll spend the quarter)

1. **Capability landscape (lightweight)** — Harvest what we already have in EOS Vision / Accountability (~50%+ if it’s there), gap-fill to two levels max, org-neutral. Purpose: see limits to profitable growth — not a dept/ERP rewrite.
2. **AI on existing Rocks** — Identify **2–3 current Rocks** where AI assist is high-leverage; for each, add the **critical few** certified docs/metrics. EOS language only — no parallel “opportunity program.”
3. **AI tooling baseline** — Vendor dashboards and agreements first (seats, spend, security/privacy). Shadow-AI: ask MSP what visibility already exists; no greenfield network monitor in this plan.
4. **Business & Ops IT** — Corporate **MSP** + separate **NetSuite host** (production runs on paper + NetSuite; shopfloor OT is minimal). Cadence on both; Scorecard for visibility. **No vendor swap** unless it becomes a Rock.

## Design rules I’ll hold
- **Tier by job, not brand** — frontier models for consequential agentic work; right-sized tools (e.g. daily Grok Bot–class assist) for routine work.
- **Certified slices** — agents only ground on the critical few sources we take pains to get right; rest stays out of the agent path.
- **In-stride, not disruptive** — ride Rocks and EOS; don’t invent a second operating system.
