# Project Charter: QCo AI Adoption Strategy

## Purpose

Frame a high-level AI adoption strategy for QCo so the AI Council can decide how the company should use AI across business capabilities. The Director of AI Technology (a new role) will select appropriate AI tools for employees and establish coaching and governance so QCo can adopt AI safely and productively. Specific products and tooling build-out are downstream of council adoption — this project is the strategy framing and presentation, not implementation.

## Users

- **QCo AI Council** (primary audience): CEO; CFO; Senior Vice President of Marketing; Chief Innovation Officer; Chief Power Engineer — need a clear strategy (capability map + ranked opportunity quadrants), tool/policy recommendations, and a measurable adoption path they can approve.
- **Director of AI Technology** (author / owner): reports to the CFO; no CTO or CIO today; role may evolve into CTO — needs a durable strategy artifact to guide tool selection, coaching, and governance.
- **QCo employees** (downstream beneficiaries): need approved tools, clear usage/security policy, and coaching for personal productivity — not direct readers of this strategy doc.

## Success Criteria

- [ ] AI Council adopts the strategy: capability map plus ranked opportunity quadrants
- [ ] Published tool matrix and usage/security policy (no AI training on company data; zero data retention)
- [ ] Measurement cadence visible to the CFO (adoption, productivity, risk incidents)
- [ ] Breadth-first personal productivity underway; depth-first automations only from high-impact, low-risk quadrants
- [ ] Adoption strategy packaged for council within 30 days
- [ ] ~50% employee adoption of AI tools within 90 days

## Scope

### In Scope

- High-level strategy framing for AI Council consideration
- Capability map across QCo business capabilities
- Ranked opportunity quadrants (impact vs risk)
- Tool matrix and usage/security policy recommendations
- Coaching and governance model outline
- CFO-facing measurement cadence design

### Out of Scope

- Building specific products or custom tools (downstream of adoption)
- Vendor procurement / contract negotiation (informed by strategy later)
- Depth-first automations outside high-impact, low-risk quadrants
- Standing up CTO/CIO org structure (may evolve later)

### To Be Determined

- Exact tool vendors and cost envelope over time
- Detailed coaching curriculum and rollout sequencing by function
- Risk-incident taxonomy and escalation paths

## Constraints

- **Timeline:** adoption strategy within 30 days; ~50% employee AI-tool adoption within 90 days
- **Budget:** not a primary constraint now; tool costs will be reviewed as options are selected
- **Policy baseline:** no AI training on company data; zero data retention
- **Org:** Director of AI Technology reports to CFO; no CTO/CIO currently

## Timeline

- **0–30 days:** strategy package for AI Council (capability map, opportunity quadrants, tool matrix, policy, measurement cadence)
- **0–90 days:** breadth-first personal productivity push toward ~50% employee adoption of approved AI tools

## Team

- Director of AI Technology (Chris) — strategy owner; reports to CFO
- QCo AI Council — decision makers

## Risks

- Council delays adoption → strategy sits without mandate; mitigate with clear decision asks and CFO-aligned metrics
- Breadth-first adoption without policy → risk incidents; mitigate by shipping tool matrix + security policy with the strategy
- Depth-first automation too early → high-risk/low-value work; mitigate with quadrant gate

## Alternatives Considered

- **Product/build-first:** rejected — products and tools are downstream of an adopted strategy
- **CTO-led tech org first:** deferred — no CTO/CIO today; Director of AI may evolve into that role over time
